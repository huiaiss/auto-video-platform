ABOUT

About Longjiang Automation

Taizhou Longjiang Automation Equipment Co., Ltd., founded in 2011, is a source factory integrating R&D, manufacturing, and sales of winding machines.

We specialize in brushless motor winding machines, magnet inserters, and paper inserting machines with 10+ years of expertise, with dozens of mature equipment models. Our products are exported to 20+ countries worldwide. We adhere to the mission of "Making Winding Smarter", providing efficient and stable automation solutions for global motor manufacturers.
